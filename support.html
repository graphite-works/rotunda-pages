About Rotunda

Rotunda is a free, general-audience reference app for the U.S. Congress. It shows recorded votes, bill text, and bill search results pulled live from official government sources (congress.gov, the U.S. Senate, and GovInfo). Rotunda has no user accounts, no ads, and no tracking — see the Privacy Policy for details.

Common questions

  • How do I clear the bills and votes I've saved? Remove an individual saved item from within the app at any time, or uninstall the app to clear everything Rotunda has stored on your device.
  • Why does bill search need an internet connection? Bill search sends your search text directly to GovInfo's public search service so it can return matching bills — Rotunda has no server of its own in that path.
  • The app isn't showing current data — what happened? Rotunda reads live from congress.gov, the Senate, and GovInfo. If one of those sources is temporarily unreachable, Rotunda may show a short-lived cached result (up to 24 hours old) until the source is reachable again.
  • Do I need an account to use Rotunda? No. Rotunda has no accounts, login, or sign-up of any kind.

Reporting a problem

To report a bug or request help, open an issue on this repository's GitHub Issues page: https://github.com/graphite-works/rotunda-pages/issues. Please include your device type, OS version, and app version, along with a description of what happened.
